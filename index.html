The short version

Why Inuvik
keeps coming back

01 — Made to order

Fresh dough, daily.

Pizza sauce made in-house, garlic sauce from scratch, dough rolled every morning — the kitchen's own words to the Inuvik Drum (Aug 2022). This is a fry-up joint that still cooks like it means it.

02 — Open late

Til midnight. Every night.

When the temperature drops and the town goes quiet, the grill at MacKenzie Road is still hot. One of the few kitchens in the Western Arctic still taking orders at 11pm.

03 — A local room

It stayed open through COVID.

While much of the hospitality industry shut its doors, The Roost kept its delivery-oriented kitchen running the entire pandemic (Inuvik Drum, Aug 2022). Inuvik noticed.
