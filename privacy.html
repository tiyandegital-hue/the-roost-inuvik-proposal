Privacy Policy

The short version: this website collects almost nothing about you. There are no accounts, no forms, no newsletter, no online ordering. If you call us, that's a phone call — between you and the kitchen.

What we collect

Nothing directly. This site has no contact form, no analytics, and sets no cookies of its own. Your browser may keep its own ordinary records (like history), which we never see.

Third parties on this site

Fonts load from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). Google may log that request the way it logs any web request — see Google's own privacy policy. Outbound links (Facebook, Instagram, Tripadvisor, Google Maps) go to those services, which have their own policies.

Phone calls

Calling +1 867-777-2727 is a normal phone call to the restaurant. We don't record calls and we don't sell phone numbers — we just take your order.

Children

This site is a restaurant menu and information site; it is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
